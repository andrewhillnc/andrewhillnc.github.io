Overview

Why buyers look at McGee’s Crossroads

McGee’s Crossroads sits along the NC-210 corridor at I-40 in northern Johnston County. Homes here carry Benson, Angier, Four Oaks, and Willow Spring mailing addresses.

Buyers come here for land. An acre here usually costs less than a much smaller lot closer to Raleigh, and many newer homes are built on lots of an acre or more. Downtown Raleigh is roughly a half-hour drive up I-40, depending on traffic.

Groceries and everyday errands are at the I-40 interchange, and larger shopping is a short drive up I-40 toward the I-40 and I-42 corridor.

History

From tobacco country to homesites

For generations this was a farming crossroads in northern Johnston County, named for the families who worked the land. Tobacco was the main crop.

When I-40 came through, commuting to Raleigh became practical, and farmland began converting to homesites. That conversion is still underway, which is why land here still costs less than comparable land closer to Raleigh.

Questions

Common questions about buying in McGee’s Crossroads

What do homes and land cost in McGee’s Crossroads right now?

Prices change month to month, so I send current numbers on request instead of posting figures that go out of date. Request the market report and I will send recent sales, homes for sale, and days on market for the area you are considering.

How far is McGee’s Crossroads from Raleigh?

Downtown Raleigh is roughly a half-hour drive up I-40, depending on the address and traffic. Test the commute at rush hour before you buy.

Do homes here have well and septic?

Many homes on acreage use a private well and septic system. Plan for well testing, a septic inspection, and a survey during due diligence, which usually means a longer due diligence period.

Which schools serve McGee’s Crossroads?

The area is in Johnston County Public Schools, with McGee’s Crossroads Elementary and Middle at the center of the community. Verify the assignment for any address with the district before making an offer.

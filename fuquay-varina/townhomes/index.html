What townhomes in Fuquay-Varina have sold for

Wake County recorded 455 qualified sales of townhouses and condos inside the Fuquay-Varina town limits between November 1, 2024 and October 5, 2026. A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are generally left out. Of the 455 sales, 447 were townhouses and 8 were condos.

The median sale price was $336,000, and the median price per heated square foot was $179. Sales ranged from $230,000 to $546,000, and the middle 80 percent fell between $283,500 and $428,800. The homes ranged from 804 to 2,906 heated square feet, with a median of 1,980, and were built between 1985 and 2026.

Most townhome sales were new construction

Homes built in 2024 or later made up 396 of the 455 sales. Their median price was slightly below the median for homes built from 2000 to 2023, and so was their median price per square foot. The 9 sales of townhomes built before 2000 were smaller homes, with a median of 1,026 square feet. Before you buy

What to check before you buy a townhome in Fuquay-Varina

Ask for the HOA documents early in your due diligence period. Read the current dues, any special assessments, and what the dues cover. Townhome associations differ on whether they handle roofs, exterior paint, siding, and lawn care, and on whether the association's insurance covers the building exterior or you need your own policy for it. The covenants also set any limits on renting the home out.

For a new townhome, read the builder's purchase contract and warranty before you sign, and have the home inspected even though it is new. For a resale, ask how old the roof and HVAC system are and whether the HOA has scheduled any exterior work.

Check the FEMA flood map for the address, especially for a home that backs to a creek, pond, or open space. My North Carolina due diligence guide covers how the due diligence fee, earnest money, and deadlines work.

School assignments are set by the Wake County Public School System and can change as new schools open, so look up the assignment for the specific address. School ratings and test scores are published by the state on NC School Report Cards and by GreatSchools.

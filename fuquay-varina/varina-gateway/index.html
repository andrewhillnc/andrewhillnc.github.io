Overview

What is in Varina Gateway

Varina Gateway is a subdivision inside the Fuquay-Varina town limits, in ZIP code 27526. Its plats were recorded with Wake County in 2022 and 2023. The homes that sold over the past two years are on Sugar Cone Way, Apple Churn Drive, Andrade Drive, and Cherry Top Drive.

Most homes that sold here are townhouses. Of the 68 qualified sales between November 2024 and October 2026, 64 were townhouses and 4 were detached houses, based on Wake County's design style for each home. The townhouses that sold were built from 2022 to 2025, and the median lot was 0.06 acres.

Sale data

What homes in Varina Gateway have sold for

Wake County recorded 64 qualified townhouse sales in Varina Gateway between November 5, 2024 and June 26, 2026. A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are generally left out. The median sale price was $415,000, and the median price per heated square foot was $172. Sales ranged from $301,500 to $492,500. The townhouses ranged from 2,254 to 2,686 heated square feet, with a median of 2,453.

Before you buy

What to check on a home in Varina Gateway

Ask for the HOA documents early in your due diligence period. Read the current dues, any special assessments, and what the dues cover. Townhouse and detached homes in the same subdivision can carry different dues and exterior maintenance rules, so read the documents for the specific home. The covenants also set any limits on renting the home out. My North Carolina due diligence guide covers how the due diligence fee, earnest money, and deadlines work.

School assignments are set by the Wake County Public School System and can change as new schools open, so look up the assignment for the specific address. School ratings and test scores are published by the state on NC School Report Cards and by GreatSchools.

Check the FEMA flood map for the address, especially for a home that backs to a creek, pond, or open space.

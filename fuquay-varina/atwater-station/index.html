Overview

What is in Atwater Station

Atwater Station is a subdivision inside the Fuquay-Varina town limits, in ZIP code 27526. The sales in the county file come from phases 1, 2, 3, and 6. The homes that sold over the past two years are on White Rail Drive, Adrift Road, Fireship Road, Ballaster Road, Flagship Road, and Safe Harbour Road.

The county file shows 25 qualified townhouse sales and 17 qualified detached house sales here, and every one was a home built in 2025 or 2026, so most sales were new construction. The townhouses that sold ranged from 2,152 to 2,626 heated square feet. The detached houses ranged from 2,120 to 3,575 heated square feet on a median lot of 0.08 acres. Before you buy

What to check on a home in Atwater Station

Ask for the HOA documents early in your due diligence period. Read the current dues, any special assessments, what the dues cover, and the covenants on fences, sheds, pools, and exterior changes. Ask whether the Atwater Station association is part of a larger master association with its own dues. For a townhouse, ask what the association maintains and insures, such as roofs, exterior walls, and lawns, and what the owner is responsible for. For a home bought from a builder, read the purchase contract and warranty before you sign, and have the home inspected even though it is new. My North Carolina due diligence guide covers how the due diligence fee, earnest money, and deadlines work.

School assignments are set by the Wake County Public School System and can change as new schools open, so look up the assignment for the specific address. School ratings and test scores are published by the state on NC School Report Cards and by GreatSchools.

Check the FEMA flood map for the address, especially for a home that backs to a creek, pond, or open space.

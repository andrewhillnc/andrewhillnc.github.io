Overview

Why buyers look at Fuquay-Varina

Fuquay-Varina is a town of about 48,500 people (U.S. Census Bureau estimate, July 2025) in southern Wake County, just south of Holly Springs. US-401, NC-42, and NC-55 all run through it, which connects it to Raleigh, Apex, Cary, and Garner.

Buyers usually come here for more land and more house for the money than they can get in the towns to the north. Land prices have generally run lower here than in Holly Springs, Apex, and Cary, and new neighborhoods continue to open along the US-401 corridor.

The town also has two historic downtowns with restaurants, breweries, and shops in the original brick buildings. Homes within walking distance of either downtown are a separate market from the newer subdivisions, and they sell differently.

Parts of Town

Where to look within Fuquay-Varina

Downtown Fuquay

The Main Street side of town, near Fuquay Mineral Spring Park. Older homes on established lots sit within walking distance of restaurants and breweries.

Varina Depot District

The second downtown, built around the railroad about a mile from Main Street. It has its own restaurants and brewery and a mix of older homes and infill.

US-401 Corridor

Most of the new construction is along US-401 heading toward Holly Springs and Raleigh. This is where builders are active and where buyers find newer homes with HOA amenities.

Willow Spring Side

The eastern side of the area, served by Willow Spring High. Lots are often larger, and some properties are on private well and septic, which affects inspections and the due diligence timeline.

Buying new construction in Fuquay-Varina

Builders are active across Fuquay-Varina, and the communities selling, the incentives offered, and the lots left change month to month. I keep that list current and send it on request instead of posting it here.

The builder’s agent works for the builder

The sales agent in the model home represents the builder. You can bring your own agent. Many builders require that your agent come with you or register you on your first visit, so contact me before you tour.

Compare incentives as a total

Incentives are often tied to the builder’s preferred lender or title company. Compare the full cost, including rate, closing costs, and upgrades, against an outside lender before you choose.

Read the builder’s contract

Builders usually use their own purchase agreement, and its terms on deposits, delays, and changes differ from a resale contract. Read it before you sign, and ask an attorney about anything you do not understand.

Inspect a new house anyway

A new home passes local code inspections, which check minimum standards. Hire your own inspector before drywall and again before closing, and once more before the builder warranty ends.

History

Why the town has two names

Around 1858, a mineral spring was uncovered on the Fuquay family farm, and people traveled to the area to drink from it. The spring still flows at Fuquay Mineral Spring Park, a short walk from Main Street.

Varina grew up separately around the railroad about a mile away. A local store owner named it after the pen name his future wife used on letters she wrote him during the Civil War.

Fuquay Springs and Varina merged into one town in 1963. That is why the name has a hyphen and the town has two downtowns.

Restaurants, schools, and parks

Eat and drink

  • Aviator Brewing: taproom, smokehouse, and SideBar
  • The Mason Jar Tavern: Southern food on Main Street, with the Mason Jar Lager Co. taproom next door
  • Vicious Fishes: brewery and kitchen on the Varina side
  • Fainting Goat Brewing: brewery in downtown Fuquay
  • Garibaldi Trattoria: Italian restaurant downtown
  • Stick Boy Bread Co.: bakery on Main Street

Schools

  • Fuquay-Varina High: the town's longtime high school
  • Willow Spring High: opened in 2021 on Old Honeycutt Road
  • Wake County Public School System assignments change as new schools open. Verify the assignment for any address with WCPSS before you make an offer. Ratings are published on NC School Report Cards.

Parks

  • Hilltop Needmore Town Park: 143 acres with ponds, open space, and about five miles of paved trails
  • Fuquay Mineral Spring Park: the spring the town started around, off Main Street

How do you get from Fuquay-Varina to Raleigh?

Fuquay-Varina is in southern Wake County, south of Holly Springs. US-401 runs north toward Raleigh, and NC-42 and NC-55 connect the town to Holly Springs, Apex, and Cary. Drive time to downtown Raleigh depends heavily on the time of day, so test the commute from a specific address at rush hour before you buy.

Which high schools serve Fuquay-Varina?

Fuquay-Varina High is the town's longtime high school, and Willow Spring High opened in 2021. WCPSS assignments change as new schools open, so verify the assignment for any specific address with WCPSS before making an offer.

Do homes around Fuquay-Varina have well and septic?

Many homes in town are on public water and sewer. Rural and semi-rural properties around Fuquay-Varina often have a private well and septic system, and those homes usually need a longer due diligence period to allow for well testing, septic inspection, and a survey.

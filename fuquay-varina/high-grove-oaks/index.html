Overview

What is in High Grove Oaks

High Grove Oaks is a subdivision inside the Fuquay-Varina town limits, in ZIP code 27526. The sales in the county file come from phases 1 through 8. The homes that sold over the past two years are on Lebrun Path, Harrell Cove Court, Boylston Drive, Hopewell Crest Drive, Clairmeade Drive, Paran Place, and Malamar Way. Wake County records the older High Grove sections as separate subdivisions, and this page does not include them.

Every qualified sale here in the county file was a detached house built between 2023 and 2026, so most sales were new construction. The homes that sold ranged from 2,797 to 5,450 heated square feet, with a median of 3,596. The median lot was 0.25 acres. Sale data

What homes in High Grove Oaks have sold for

Wake County recorded 121 qualified sales of detached houses in High Grove Oaks between November 6, 2024 and August 26, 2026, covering 120 different homes (one sold twice). A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are generally left out. The median sale price was $728,000, and the median price per heated square foot was $198. Sales ranged from $550,000 to $866,000, and the middle 80 percent fell between $626,000 and $822,500.

Before you buy

What to check on a home in High Grove Oaks

Ask for the HOA documents early in your due diligence period. Read the current dues, any special assessments, what the dues cover, and the covenants on fences, sheds, pools, and exterior changes. Ask whether the High Grove Oaks association is part of a larger master association with its own dues. For a home bought from a builder, read the purchase contract and warranty before you sign, and have the home inspected even though it is new. My North Carolina due diligence guide covers how the due diligence fee, earnest money, and deadlines work.

School assignments are set by the Wake County Public School System and can change as new schools open, so look up the assignment for the specific address. School ratings and test scores are published by the state on NC School Report Cards and by GreatSchools.

Check the FEMA flood map for the address, especially for a home that backs to a creek, pond, or open space.

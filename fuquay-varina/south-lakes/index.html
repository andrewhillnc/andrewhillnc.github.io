Overview

What is in South Lakes

South Lakes is a subdivision inside the Fuquay-Varina town limits, in ZIP code 27526. The sales in the county file come from several plats recorded under the South Lakes name, including parcels SF-7, SF-8, SF-11, and AR-1. Its streets are named for lakes. The detached homes that sold over the past two years are on 28 streets, with the most sales on Bailey Lake Drive, Apalachia Lake Drive, Jones Lake Road, Lake Artesia Lane, and Glenville Lake Drive. Holding Village South Lake in Wake Forest and South Lake in Wake County's unincorporated area are different subdivisions, and this page does not include them.

Most qualified sales here were detached houses. The ones that sold were built between 2007 and 2025, ranged from 1,319 to 3,826 heated square feet with a median of 2,840, and sat on a median lot of 0.2 acres. Of the 121 detached sales, 32 were homes built in 2024 or 2025. The county file also shows 8 townhouse sales on Bailey Lake Drive, all built in 2026.

What homes in South Lakes have sold for

Detached houses

Wake County recorded 121 qualified sales of detached houses in South Lakes between November 20, 2024 and October 1, 2026, covering 113 different homes (some sold more than once). A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are generally left out. The median sale price was $560,000, and the median price per heated square foot was $203. Sales ranged from $236,000 to $875,000.

Before you buy

What to check on a home in South Lakes

Ask for the HOA documents early in your due diligence period. Read the current dues, any special assessments, what the dues cover, and the covenants on fences, sheds, pools, and exterior changes. Ask whether the South Lakes association is part of a larger master association with its own dues. For a townhouse, ask what the association maintains and insures, such as roofs, exterior walls, and lawns, and what the owner is responsible for. For a resale home, ask the seller for the age of the roof, HVAC system, and water heater, and have the home inspected. For a home bought from a builder, read the purchase contract and warranty before you sign, and have the home inspected even though it is new. My North Carolina due diligence guide covers how the due diligence fee, earnest money, and deadlines work.

School assignments are set by the Wake County Public School System and can change as new schools open, so look up the assignment for the specific address. School ratings and test scores are published by the state on NC School Report Cards and by GreatSchools.

Check the FEMA flood map for the address, especially for a home that backs to a creek, pond, or open space.

Overview

Why buyers look at Chapel Hill

Chapel Hill is a town of about 63,600 people (U.S. Census Bureau estimate, July 2025), mostly in Orange County. The University of North Carolina and UNC Health are its largest employers, and Franklin Street runs along the edge of campus.

Buyers should plan for a few costs up front. Orange County property taxes run higher than Wake County’s, and the Chapel Hill–Carrboro City Schools district is a large part of what buyers pay for here. Inventory is limited and much of the housing is older.

The county line matters. Some homes with a Chapel Hill mailing address sit in Chatham County, where property taxes are lower and schools are part of Chatham County Schools instead of Chapel Hill–Carrboro.

Neighborhoods

Where to look in Chapel Hill

Laurel Hill and Morgan Creek

Wooded ridges near the NC Botanical Garden, with large lots and mid-century homes. Homes here sometimes sell before they are publicly listed.

The Oaks

An established neighborhood around Chapel Hill Country Club, close to campus and the hospital.

Meadowmont and Southern Village

Two planned communities built around walkable centers. Meadowmont is on the Durham side of town, and Southern Village is south on US 15-501. They are the usual choice for buyers who want newer homes in Chapel Hill–Carrboro schools.

Governors Club

A gated golf community with a Chapel Hill mailing address that sits in Chatham County. Compare property taxes and school assignment against Orange County homes before you choose.

The Jordan Lake Side

South and west into Chatham County, including Fearrington Village and Briar Chapel. Jordan Lake covers about 13,900 acres. Homes here are in Chatham County Schools.

If you are comparing homes on both sides of the county line, call or text me and I will send recent sales along with the tax and school differences for each.

History

A town built around the university

The University of North Carolina was chartered in 1789 and was the first public university in the United States to open its doors. The town of Chapel Hill grew up around it.

Franklin Street, the main street along campus, is named for Benjamin Franklin. More than two centuries later, the university and UNC Health are still the town’s economic base.

Common questions about buying in Chapel Hill

What do homes cost in Chapel Hill right now?

Prices change month to month, so I send current numbers on request instead of posting figures that go out of date. Request the market report and I will send recent sales, homes for sale, and days on market for the area you are considering.

Why do Chapel Hill homes in Chatham County cost less in taxes?

Property taxes in Chatham County run lower than in Orange County. Homes there can keep a Chapel Hill mailing address, but they are in Chatham County Schools, so check both taxes and school assignment before comparing listings.

Which school district is a Chapel Hill address in?

Most of Chapel Hill is in Chapel Hill–Carrboro City Schools. Some addresses are in Orange County Schools, and Chatham County addresses are in Chatham County Schools. Verify the district for any specific address before making an offer.

Where can I find newer homes in Chapel Hill?

Meadowmont and Southern Village are the main planned communities with newer homes in Chapel Hill–Carrboro schools. Briar Chapel in Chatham County has newer construction with a Chapel Hill mailing address and Chatham County schools.

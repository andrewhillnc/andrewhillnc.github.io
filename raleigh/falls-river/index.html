Overview

What the county records show for Falls River

Wake County records list these homes on Falls River plats, including the Falls River townhouse and condo plats. Bedford at Falls River and The Enclave at Fall River are recorded as separate subdivisions and are not included in the figures on this page.

All 84 Falls River homes that sold in the past two years are inside Raleigh's city limits in ZIP code 27614. They were built between 1996 and 2003 and range from 927 to 4,189 heated square feet. Homes that sold are on streets including Falls River Avenue, Cokesbury Lane, Farmington Grove Drive, River Bank Drive, Neland Street, and Southwalk Lane.

Sale data

What homes in Falls River have sold for

Wake County recorded 89 qualified sales of 84 homes in Falls River between November 21, 2024 and October 16, 2026. A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are left out. The median sale price was $465,000, and the median price per heated square foot was $249. Sales ranged from $206,000 to $935,000.

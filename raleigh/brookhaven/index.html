Overview

What the county records show for Brookhaven

Wake County records list Brookhaven in numbered sections, extensions, and annexes, such as Brookhaven Extension 1 and Brookhaven Annex C, and the figures on this page include all of them. Olde Brookhaven, Pines of Brookhaven, The Reserve at Brookhaven, Brookhaven Estates, and Brookhaven West are recorded as separate subdivisions and are not included.

All 34 Brookhaven homes that sold in the past two years are inside Raleigh's city limits in ZIP code 27612. They were built between 1960 and 2006, and the median year built was 1967. They range from 1,735 to 5,320 heated square feet. Homes that sold are on streets including Brookhaven Drive, Rembert Drive, Winthrop Drive, Larchmont Drive, Dodsworth Drive, and Shamrock Drive.

Sale data

What homes in Brookhaven have sold for

Wake County recorded 34 qualified sales of 34 homes in Brookhaven between November 12, 2024 and July 27, 2026. The county file runs through October 30, 2026 and shows no qualified Brookhaven sales after July 27. A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are left out. The median sale price was $712,500, and the median price per heated square foot was $250. Sales ranged from $400,000 to $1,390,000.

Qualified sales by calendar year. 2024 covers November 12 to December 31; 2026 covers January 1 to July 27. With this few sales per year, the median moves a lot with the mix of homes that sold.

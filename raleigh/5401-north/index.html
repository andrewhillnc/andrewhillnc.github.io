Overview

What is in 5401 North

Wake County's July 2026 residential report counts 715 homes on 5401 North plats, all inside Raleigh's planning area in ZIP code 27616. The county counts townhouses as single-family buildings, so that total includes both townhouses and detached houses. Apartment buildings in the community are not included.

The neighborhood is new. Of the 715 homes, 364 were built in the 2010s and 351 in the 2020s. The middle 80 percent of homes have between 1,708 and 2,896 heated square feet, and the median is 2,089. Lots are small: the median is 0.08 acres, and most fall between 0.03 and 0.15 acres.

From Andrew

My advice on buying in 5401 North

I have lived in 5401 North. These are the points I walk buyers through before they make an offer here.

Buyers have room to negotiate

As of November 2026, many homes in the community are for sale at the same time. That gives buyers leverage on price and terms, so do not assume you need to offer at or above the asking price.

Townhouses: choose a two-story end unit

If you are buying a townhouse as an investment, I recommend a two-story end unit. The townhouses on Wallace Martin Way are the ones I point buyers to first. I steer buyers away from the three-story townhouses on Perry Creek Road. Perry Creek Road is the main street through the community, and in my experience those units resell for less. Over the past two years, Wake County recorded a median townhouse sale price of $335,000 on Perry Creek Road, compared with $369,000 on Kayton Street.

Detached homes: look on the east side of Perry Creek Road

For a detached house, I recommend the section on the east side of Perry Creek Road. Homes there have access to the Neuse River Trail.

Test the Louisburg Road commute

Traffic on Louisburg Road can be heavy at evening rush hour on the way back into the community. Before you buy, drive the route from your work at the time you would normally come home. Construction in the surrounding area is ongoing, and I expect it to continue, so plan for road work and new building near the community for the next several years.

Budget for rising HOA dues

Townhouse HOA dues have risen to about $230 a month and continue to go up. Dues for detached homes are over $100 a month. Confirm the current amount for the specific home in the HOA documents before you make an offer.

Check the rental rules

The recorded declaration for the community (Wake County Register of Deeds, Book 16242, Page 22) requires every lease to be in writing with a term of at least one year. The owner must send the HOA a copy of the lease, and a security deposit equal to 30 percent of one month's rent must be paid to the HOA. The declaration sets a fine of $100 for each day a home is leased in violation of these rules.

The HOA also limits the number of homes that can be rented and requires a rental permit. In my view these rules have held prices down, because investors are less willing to buy where they cannot rent freely. If you might rent the home later, ask the HOA for its current rental policy before you make an offer.

What is in the community

The community has resort-style amenities. Smooth Joe Coffee and Heyday Brewing are both on Tin Roof Way, inside the neighborhood.

Sale data

What homes in 5401 North have sold for

Wake County recorded 63 qualified sales of homes in 5401 North between November 4, 2024 and October 29, 2026. A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are left out. The median sale price was $425,000, and the median price per heated square foot was $189. Sales ranged from $298,000 to $650,000.

For sellers

Selling a home in 5401 North

With this many homes for sale in the community, exposure decides whether yours sells. A home that goes on the MLS and waits for buyers will sit. I use a plan built on two things: pricing against the competition and putting the listing in front of more buyers.

Price against what is listed today

Buyers in 5401 North compare every similar home for sale at the same time, and they go to the best value first. I set the list price against the homes currently on the market, not against last year's sales. A home priced even slightly above its competition will sit while buyers tour the others. I will show you the current listings you are competing with and the price I would recommend.

Give the listing more exposure with Zillow Showcase

I list homes here with Zillow Showcase, Zillow's premium listing format. A Showcase listing includes an interactive floor plan, a virtual tour, and placement on Zillow that standard listings do not get. Zillow reports that fewer than 1 percent of its listings use Showcase, so it sets a home apart in a community where buyers are scrolling through many similar options.

Zillow's own data reports that active Showcase listings received 79 percent more page views and 76 percent more saves than similar listings without it, and that Showcase homes were more likely to go under contract within 14 days and sold for about 2 percent more. Those figures come from Zillow's comparisons of similar homes over the 12 months before April 2025 (Zillow Showcase facts).

Before you buy

What to check on a home in 5401 North

Ask the seller for the HOA documents early in your due diligence period, including the current dues, any special assessments, and what the dues cover. Townhouse and detached homes can carry different dues and exterior maintenance rules, so read the documents for the specific home. My North Carolina due diligence guide covers how the fee, earnest money, and deadlines work.

School assignments are set by the Wake County Public School System and can change, so look up the assignment for the specific address. School ratings and test scores are published by the state on NC School Report Cards and by GreatSchools. Check the FEMA flood map for any home near a creek or pond.

Overview

What is in 12 Oaks

Wake County records list the subdivision as Twelve Oaks, and most people write it as 12 Oaks. The county's July 2026 residential report counts 1,705 single-family homes on Twelve Oaks plats, all inside the Holly Springs planning area. A small share of the sales below are townhouses, which the county counts as single-family buildings.

Most of the neighborhood was built in the last 15 years. Of the 1,705 homes, 1,155 were built in the 2010s, 492 in the 2020s, and 58 in the 2000s. The middle 80 percent of homes have between 2,246 and 4,133 heated square feet, and the median is 2,949. The median lot is 0.19 acres, and most lots fall between 0.12 and 0.35 acres.

The community is built around The Club at 12 Oaks, a private club with a Nicklaus Design golf course. Club membership terms and costs come from the club, so ask the club directly and ask the seller which club or HOA fees come with a specific home.

Sale data

What homes in 12 Oaks have sold for

Wake County recorded 178 qualified sales in 12 Oaks between November 4, 2024 and October 6, 2026. A qualified sale is one a county appraiser reviewed as an arm's-length transaction, so foreclosures and sales between relatives are left out. The median sale price was $757,500, and the median price per heated square foot was $265. Sales ranged from $433,000 to $2,125,000, and 44 of the 178 sold for $1 million or more.

Before you buy

What to check on a home in 12 Oaks

Ask the seller for the HOA documents early in your due diligence period, including the current dues, any special assessments, and the rules for fences, sheds, and exterior changes. Club membership is a separate question: ask The Club at 12 Oaks what membership options exist and what they cost before you write an offer.

For homes next to the golf course or a pond, check the FEMA flood map for the exact address, since flood insurance requirements change the monthly cost of owning the home. My North Carolina due diligence guide covers how the fee, earnest money, and deadlines work.

School assignments in Holly Springs are set by the Wake County Public School System and can change as new schools open, so look up the assignment for the specific address. School ratings and test scores are published by the state on NC School Report Cards and by GreatSchools.

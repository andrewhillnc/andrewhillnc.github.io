Overview

Why buyers look at Garner

Garner is a town in southern Wake County, directly south of Raleigh. The Census Bureau estimated its population at about 41,600 in 2025, up from 31,159 in the 2020 census. That is roughly a third more people in five years.

In September 2024, the Toll NC-540 Triangle Expressway opened 18 miles from the NC-55 Bypass in Apex to the I-40/I-42 interchange in Garner. That gave Garner a direct toll-road route west toward Holly Springs, Apex, and Research Triangle Park. A second phase of about 10 miles will run from Garner to Knightdale, with completion expected in late 2028.

Amazon opened a fulfillment center in Garner in 2020. The town’s other large employers have included Pepsi Bottling Ventures and Sigma Electric, along with Wake County schools and major retailers.

Parts of Town

Where to look within Garner

Downtown and Main Street

The original railroad town. The Downtown Garner Historic District is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, and the older homes nearby sit on established lots.

North Garner and the US-70 Corridor

US-70 is the main east-west road and carries most of the town’s shopping. Neighborhoods here are the closest part of Garner to downtown Raleigh.

Near I-40 and NC-540

Homes near the I-40/I-42 interchange have the quickest access to NC-540 west and I-40 toward Raleigh or Clayton. Commuters to RTP and Holly Springs usually start here.

South Toward Lake Benson and NC-50

NC-50 runs south from here toward Johnston County. This side of town is closest to Lake Benson Park and White Deer Park.

Neighborhoods

Neighborhoods buyers ask about in Garner

These are the subdivisions that come up most often when buyers search Garner. I send current sales for any of them on request.

Heather Woods

Homes built from about 1980 to 1990, roughly 2,200 to 3,700 square feet, on lots with mature trees. Sidewalks connect to White Deer Park, Lake Benson Park, and the Poole Family YMCA. There is no HOA.

Lake Benson and Autumn Oaks

Mostly brick single-family homes from the early 2000s, about 2,500 to 3,200 square feet on lots of roughly a third to half an acre. Lake Benson Park and White Deer Park are close by.

The Village at Aversboro

A 55-and-older community started around 2008, with 147 single-family homes of about 1,900 square feet and a clubhouse. The HOA maintains the yards, and Lake Benson Park is within walking distance.

Creekside

Built around 1999 and 2000. Homes run about 1,600 to 2,600 square feet, with established trees and a neighborhood association.

Turner Farms

Homes built from the mid-1980s through 2017, many on larger or wooded lots. Some of the older homes have been renovated.

Chadbourne

Brick homes from the early 2000s onward, roughly 3,000 to over 5,000 square feet on lots of three-quarters of an acre or more, near the NC-50 and NC-42 intersection. Pool, tennis courts, and clubhouse.

White Oak Area

Newer townhomes and single-family homes around the NC-42 and I-40/I-42 interchange, next to White Oak Shopping Center.

Cleveland Area: Check the County

Adams Point, Kyndal, Cleveland Bluffs, and Summerwind Plantation often show a Garner mailing address but sit in Johnston County. That changes the school system and the property tax rate, so confirm the county on the tax record before you compare prices with in-town Garner.

History

From railroad stop to Raleigh suburb

Garner began as a wood-and-water stop on the North Carolina Railroad, which was built through the area in the 1850s. Thomas Bingham, the first postmaster, asked for a post office named Garner’s Station around 1878.

Garner’s Station was incorporated in 1883, but its charter was repealed in 1891. The town was incorporated again in 1905 as the Town of Garner after it got a railroad depot. In the early 1950s, US-70 was rerouted around downtown, and new commercial building moved to shopping centers along the highway.

Living Here

Schools, parks, and things to do

Schools

  • Garner Magnet High and South Garner High serve the town.
  • Garner is in the Wake County Public School System. Assignments change as new schools open, so verify the assignment for any address with WCPSS before you make an offer.

Parks and trails

  • Lake Benson Park on Buffaloe Road, beside Lake Benson
  • White Deer Park on Aversboro Road
  • Garner Recreational Park on East Garner Road
  • More than four miles of paved town trails

Getting around

  • I-40 along the north and east sides of town, toward Raleigh and Clayton
  • Toll NC-540 west toward Holly Springs and Apex from the I-40/I-42 interchange
  • US-70 east–west through town; US-401 and NC-50 north–south

Questions

Common questions about buying in Garner

What do homes cost in Garner right now?

Prices change month to month, so I send current numbers on request instead of posting figures that go out of date. Request the market report and I will send recent sales, homes for sale, and days on market for the area you are considering.

How do you get from Garner to Raleigh and RTP?

I-40 and US-70 run north into Raleigh. Toll NC-540 runs west from the I-40/I-42 interchange toward Holly Springs, Apex, and RTP. Drive times depend on the time of day, so test the commute from a specific address at rush hour before you buy.

Which high schools serve Garner?

Garner Magnet High and South Garner High serve most of the town. WCPSS assignments change as new schools open, so verify the assignment for any specific address with WCPSS before making an offer.

Should I look in Garner or Clayton?

Garner is in Wake County and closer to Raleigh. Clayton is east on I-40 in Johnston County, which has its own school system and its own property tax rate. I can send recent sales from both so you can compare similar homes side by side.

Which One Fits

How buyers usually decide

More land for the money

Land prices have generally run lower in Fuquay-Varina than in Holly Springs, so buyers can often get more land per dollar by moving a few miles south. Lot sizes in the newer subdivisions are similar in both towns.

Commuting to RTP

Holly Springs homes near the NC-540 interchanges have the fastest route north toward Research Triangle Park.

Working at Amgen or Fujifilm

Both plants are in Holly Springs, so homes in Holly Springs or northern Fuquay-Varina keep the drive short.

A walkable downtown

Fuquay-Varina has two historic downtowns with restaurants and breweries. Holly Springs has one along NC-55.

The Sunset Lake area

Neighborhoods around Sunset Lake sit near the line between the two towns. Check which town the address is in, because town taxes and services differ.

Well and septic

Many homes outside the Fuquay-Varina town limits use a private well and septic system, which adds inspections and time to your due diligence. Read my due diligence guide.

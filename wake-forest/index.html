Overview

Why buyers look at Wake Forest

Wake Forest is a town of about 58,100 people (U.S. Census Bureau estimate, July 2025) in northern Wake County, along US-1. It offers a historic downtown on White Street and larger lots than buyers usually find closer to Raleigh.

Homes in and next to the historic downtown have held their value most consistently. Established neighborhoods like Heritage behave differently at resale from the newest subdivisions at the edges of town, where values depend more on what gets built nearby.

West of town toward Falls Lake, much of the land sits in the protected Falls Lake watershed. Development rules there keep lots large, often three to five acres or more, which is why custom homes on acreage are still available.

Parts of Town

Where to look in Wake Forest

Historic Downtown

White Street’s brick storefronts and North Main Street, which runs past the homes of the old college faculty. The historic core is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Heritage

An established neighborhood with a golf course and its own high school nearby. Resale homes here have a track record that newer subdivisions do not.

North Along US-1 and NC-98

Where most of the new construction is going in. Lots and prices vary widely, so compare each community’s recent resales with its new-build pricing.

The Falls Lake Side

Custom homes on large wooded lots west of town. Watershed rules affect wells, septic, and what you can build or add later, so plan for that during due diligence.

History

The original home of Wake Forest University

Wake Forest College was founded here in 1834, and the town grew up around the campus. In 1956 the college moved to Winston-Salem, where it became Wake Forest University.

Southeastern Baptist Theological Seminary took over the original campus in 1956 and still uses it today. North Main Street still runs past the homes where the college’s professors lived, and the historic core was added to the National Register of Historic Places.

Because Wake Forest was an established town for well over a century before the recent growth reached it, its downtown has a different character from newer suburbs.

Common questions about buying in Wake Forest

What do homes cost in Wake Forest right now?

Prices change month to month, so I send current numbers on request instead of posting figures that go out of date. Request the market report and I will send recent sales, homes for sale, and days on market for the area you are considering.

What should I know about buying near Falls Lake?

Much of the land west of town is in the Falls Lake watershed, where development rules keep lots large. Those rules also affect wells, septic, and future additions, so plan for well testing, a septic inspection, and a survey during due diligence.

Which high schools serve Wake Forest?

Wake Forest High and Heritage High serve most of the town. WCPSS assignments change as new schools open, so verify the assignment for any specific address with WCPSS before making an offer.

How do you get from Wake Forest to Raleigh?

US-1 (Capital Boulevard) is the main route south to Raleigh. Drive times depend on the address and time of day, so test the commute at rush hour before you buy.
